Welcome to Your Neighborhood Bakery

Start your morning with a warm loaf, bring home a box of pastries, or plan ahead for your next family gathering. North Star Bakery focuses on small-batch baking, dependable quality, and friendly service for our local community.

What We're Known For

  • Crusty artisan breads baked in small batches.
  • Handmade pastries for breakfast, coffee breaks, and sharing.
  • Celebration cakes and baked goods for community events.
  • Easy pre-orders for customers who want their favorites set aside.
