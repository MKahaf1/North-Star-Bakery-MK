Breads

Our bread selection changes with the baking schedule and season. Expect hearty loaves, sandwich breads, and rustic crusts that work equally well for breakfast or dinner.

Pastries

Choose from flaky breakfast pastries, sweet treats, and rotating seasonal selections. Ask about the day's fresh-baked options when you visit.

Typical price range: $3–$7 each or $18–$30 per assorted box.

Cakes & Celebration Bakes

We prepare cakes and baked goods for birthdays, office gatherings, family celebrations, and neighborhood events. Lead times vary by size and design.

Typical price range: $35–$90 for cakes; custom event orders priced by quantity and design.
