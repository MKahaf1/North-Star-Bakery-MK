Our Story

North Star Bakery began with a simple idea: make dependable, handmade baked goods that feel at home in the neighborhood. We bake in small batches, welcome regulars by name, and build our menu around the products our customers return for again and again.

Our Sourcing Philosophy

We prioritize ingredients from regional and local suppliers whenever practical, while selecting specialty ingredients based on quality and consistency. Our goal is to know where important ingredients come from and to use them thoughtfully so the finished bake remains the focus.

Meet the People Behind the Bake

Head Baker

Leads daily production, develops seasonal breads, and keeps our signature recipes consistent from batch to batch.

Pastry Lead

Prepares our rotating pastry selection and helps create special-order desserts for celebrations and community events.

Neighborhood Counter Team

Greets customers, manages pre-orders, answers ingredient questions, and helps busy commuters and families get what they need quickly.
